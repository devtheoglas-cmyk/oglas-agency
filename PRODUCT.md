# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Prospective clients and collaborators reviewing The Oglas Agency's portfolio to understand the studio's work, craft, and range.

## Product Purpose

The Oglas Agency website presents the studio, its services, and selected client work. Case-study pages turn finished identity systems into clear, image-led stories that demonstrate the quality and breadth of the work.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines strategic context with full-scale visual applications so each project is experienced as a coherent brand system rather than a gallery of isolated mockups.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React and Vite single-page website deployed at `oglasglobal.com`.
- Case studies live at `/works/:slug` and must remain responsive, accessible, and consistent with the shared site navigation and footer.
- The Velvet Properties page inherits the established Fishwala case-study structure.
- The Gymkha page inherits the same image-led case-study structure.
- The final content section is "More work"; the global Oglas footer closes the page.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve The Oglas Agency's existing site-wide navigation, typography, work-card system, and footer.
- Preserve each featured client's own visual identity inside its case study.
- Velvet Properties uses its supplied 2025 brand guideline as the source of truth: deep green, copper/peach, white, geometric V pattern, and "The Art of Realty" positioning.
- Gymkha uses its supplied 2025 brand book as the source of truth: black and white, compressed athletic typography, the angular G mark, and "Take the First Step" positioning.

## Product Principles

- Let the work lead through large, high-fidelity imagery.
- Explain strategy briefly and only where it strengthens the visual story.
- Preserve the client's identity while keeping the Oglas portfolio experience coherent.
- Make every case study complete on desktop and mobile.
